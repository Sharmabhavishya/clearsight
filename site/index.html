    How it works
    
        
    No servers. No image code. Cloudinary does the heavy lifting.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    1
    Upload
    Your poster goes straight from the browser to Cloudinary through an unsigned preset. No secret ever touches the page.
    POST /image/upload
    
        
    2
    Transform
    Every version is just a URL. Cloudinary renders it at the edge: simulations, stripe fixes, greyscale, contrast.
    e_simulate_colorblind
    
        
    3
    Deliver
    f_auto and q_auto send each phone the lightest format it can open, cached on a global CDN.
    f_auto,q_auto:eco
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Why I built this
    
        
    Public notices exist to keep people safe. Too many are designed for one kind of reader: perfect colour vision, a fast connection, a big screen. So I built the tool that checks and fixes them in a minute.
