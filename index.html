ClearSight

Public information people can actually see.

Health notices, evacuation maps, school charts and NGO posters are often published as a single full-colour, full-size image. ClearSight uses Cloudinary to turn that one image into an accessibility pack in seconds: colour-blind-safe, low-vision, low-bandwidth and photocopy-ready versions, each with a shareable URL.

~1 in 12 menand ~1 in 200 women have colour vision deficiency
2.2 billionpeople live with a near or distance vision impairment (WHO)
2.6 billionpeople were still offline in 2024; many more are on slow, metered data (ITU)

1. Pick an image

Try a sample, or upload your own poster, map or chart to your Cloudinary account.

Upload your own image (uses your Cloudinary account)

Create an unsigned preset in Cloudinary Console → Settings → Upload → Upload presets → Add preset → Signing mode: Unsigned. No API secret ever touches the browser.

2. See it the way others do

Drag the slider to compare the original (left) with another version (right).

3. Your accessibility pack

Every version is a live Cloudinary URL, generated on demand at the edge, so nothing has to be re-designed or stored twice.

4. Publish it

Paste this into any website or CMS. Browsers on slow connections and small screens automatically get the light version.
